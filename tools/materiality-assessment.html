Materiality Assessment Worksheet

SORT YOUR IMPACTS AND RISKS INTO GRI-MATERIAL, IFRS-MATERIAL, OR BOTH

List every impact, risk, or issue your company can identify — across operations and the value chain. For each one, ask two separate questions. GRI test: is this a significant impact on people, the environment, or the economy? IFRS S1/S2 test: could this reasonably affect cash flow, financing, or the company's prospects? An issue can be material under one lens, both, or neither — mark each column honestly rather than assuming the same answer applies to both.

Reading your results

Y / Y — Material under both

Your strongest disclosure candidates. Real-world impact and could plausibly move cash flow, financing, or valuation. Prioritize these first.

Y / N — GRI-only

A genuine impact on people, environment, or economy, but no clear financial link today. Still worth disclosing under GRI.

N / Y — IFRS-only

Financially relevant but not a standout impact issue on its own. Easy to miss from a purely impact-driven mindset — review carefully.

N / N — Likely not material

Neither test is triggered. Keep a short note of why it was screened out, in case circumstances change.
