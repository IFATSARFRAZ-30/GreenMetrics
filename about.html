About GreenMetrics

GreenMetrics is Ifat Sarfraz's sustainability reporting and assurance practice — built for companies working through CSRD, IFRS S1/S2, GRI, and the assurance standards that increasingly sit behind them.

A

Background

Ifat Sarfraz is a Chartered Management Accountant (ACMA, APFA), based in Faisalabad, Pakistan. Alongside GreenMetrics, Ifat runs Ifat Associates (tax and business consulting) and Ifat & Co (accounting software, courses, and textbook development), and serves as Visiting Faculty at ICMA Pakistan.

B

Focus areas

  • Sustainability reporting under CSRD, IFRS S1/S2, GRI, TCFD, and SASB
  • Assurance readiness under ISAE 3000/3410
  • Materiality assessments and dual-materiality frameworks
  • Practical guidance for companies navigating cross-border reporting pressure — from EU-linked requirements like CBAM to buyer and investor questionnaires
C

Who this is for

Listed companies, multinationals, development-sector organizations, and exporters anywhere in the world working through sustainability reporting and assurance requirements.
