Ifat Sarfraz

Founder, GreenMetrics · Chartered Management Accountant (ACMA, APFA)

My work in ESG

I'm a Chartered Management Accountant based in Faisalabad, Pakistan, and I built GreenMetrics to bring that accounting and assurance background into sustainability reporting. My focus is IFRS S1/S2, GRI, TCFD, and SASB disclosures, and assurance readiness under ISAE 3000/3410 — helping companies and audit firms turn sustainability requirements into evidence and data they can actually stand behind, rather than a strategy document that sits on a shelf.

My vision for GreenMetrics

Sustainability reporting is becoming an accounting problem — standards, data, evidence, and disclosure, in that order. I want GreenMetrics to be the place companies and audit firms in Pakistan go to when SECP's IFRS S1/S2 requirements stop being theoretical and start being due, and from there, to become a trusted partner for international buyers and firms who need that same rigor applied to their work in Pakistan and South Asia.
