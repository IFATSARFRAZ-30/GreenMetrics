My work in ESG

I'm a Chartered Management Accountant (ACMA, APFA) based in Faisalabad, Pakistan. I built GreenMetrics to bring an accounting and assurance mindset — standards, evidence, disclosure — into sustainability reporting, rather than treating it as a communications exercise. My focus is IFRS S1/S2, GRI, TCFD, and SASB disclosures, assurance readiness under ISAE 3000/3410, and basic carbon accounting for companies that need a starting point before anything more complex.

GreenMetrics is a new practice. I'm building it in the open — the free tools, checklists, and guides on this site are the actual work, published as they're built, rather than promises about work done elsewhere.

Where I focus

01

IFRS S1 / S2

General sustainability and climate disclosures, structured for SECP's direction in Pakistan.

02

GRI

Materiality-first reporting for companies whose stakeholders expect the GRI standard.

03

Assurance readiness

Getting data and evidence into shape for ISAE 3000/3410 assurance, before the audit firm asks.

04

Carbon accounting

Basic Scope 1/2 (and early Scope 3) accounting for companies just starting to measure.

My vision for GreenMetrics

Sustainability reporting is becoming an accounting problem — standards, data, evidence, and disclosure, in that order. I want GreenMetrics to be the place companies and audit firms in Pakistan go to when SECP's IFRS S1/S2 requirements stop being theoretical and start being due, and from there, to become a trusted partner for international buyers and firms who need that same rigor applied to their work in Pakistan and South Asia.
